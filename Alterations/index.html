    Good to know

    Questions, answered.

    When should I start my alterations?

    About six to eight weeks before you need the dress. Tighter or longer timelines are fine; we’ll plan around your date.

    How many fittings will I need?

    Most dresses need two to three. Occasionally a complex gown needs one more, and we’ll tell you at the first fitting.

    How much do wedding dress alterations cost?

    Typically £360–£750 plus VAT, depending on the work. Adding sleeves, relining or intricate design changes may cost more.

    What should I bring?

    Your wedding shoes and the underwear you’ll wear on the day, to every appointment. You’re welcome to browse our veils and accessories too.
